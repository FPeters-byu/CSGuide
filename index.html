How HubSpot Organizes Data: 4 Core Objects

The Big Picture

Contacts (People)

Individual people CSG interacts with—such as prospective clients, active sponsors, partners, and alumni.

Companies (Organizations)

The businesses or organizations those contacts belong to. Everything related to a client lives under their company record.

Deals (Projects & Pipeline)

Potential and active consulting engagements tracked from initial conversation through project completion.

Tickets (Follow-Ups & Support)

Ongoing client requests, outreach threads, and post-project check-ins managed in one shared place.

2 Simple Habits for Every Team: (1) Search before creating a new contact or company to keep our database clean. (2) Log key takeaways after client meetings so future cohorts always have full context.
End-of-Semester Handover Rule (Week 13/14): (1) Confirm every graduating client has a completed NPS/Feedback Survey and pinned final summary note. (2) Reassign the Service Hub Pro Seat, Marketing Core Seats, and Sales Seats from graduating seniors to incoming leads (downgrade seniors to free View-Only access so all history stays intact).

What Your Marketing Seats Let You Do (Marketing Hub Pro)

CSG has 3 Marketing Core Seats and 2,000 Marketing Contacts. Here is what you can run:

HubSpot Menu: Marketing > Campaigns

Campaigns & Marketing Studio

What do I want to do? Run a coordinated campaign across email and LinkedIn and track generated leads.

−

What this tool does: Groups all emails, forms, social posts, and landing pages under one campaign dashboard so CSG can measure how many leads and deals each initiative produced.

  1. Go to Marketing > Campaigns (or Marketing > Marketing Studio) and click Create campaign.
  2. Name it clearly by semester and goal (e.g., Fall 2026 – Tech Startup Outreach) and set your target audience.
  3. Attach your campaign assets (forms, emails, LinkedIn posts) using the Add assets button.
  4. Review influenced contacts and new deals directly inside the campaign dashboard.
If blocked or missing: Requires one of CSG's 3 Marketing Core Seats. If Create campaign is greyed out, ask your Super-Admin to enable Campaigns & Marketing Edit permissions under Settings > Users & Teams.

HubSpot Menu: CRM > Deals

Deals & Pipeline Tracking

What do I want to do? Log a new prospect engagement and track it through our pipeline stages.

−

What this tool does: Tracks every potential client project from initial outreach to signed contract, linked directly to the Company and Contact.

  1. Go to CRM > Deals and click Create deal (top right).
  2. Name it [Company Name] – [Semester/Scope], select the CSG Pipeline, enter the deal amount, and set the expected close date.
  3. Link the Company and decision-maker Contact under Associations, then drag the deal card across stages as it progresses.
If blocked or missing: Works with any paid Pro seat (Sales Pro, Marketing Core, or Service Pro). If Create deal is greyed out, ask your Super-Admin to change your CRM Deal & Company permissions from View-Only to Edit under Settings > Users & Teams.

HubSpot Menu: Library > Meetings

Meeting Scheduling Links

What do I want to do? Let a prospect book a Discovery Call directly onto my calendar.

−

What this tool does: Syncs with your calendar (replaces Calendly) and automatically logs booked calls onto the Contact record.

HubSpot Menu: CRM > Companies & Deals (Pro Equivalent of Customer Success)

Client & Project Database (Account Health & History)

What do I want to do? Collect and update all past clients, projects, and contacts with the Operations Director.

−

What this tool does: Links every client organization (Company), decision-maker (Contact), and past consulting engagement (Deal) in one place. (Note: The dedicated Service > Customer Success workspace requires Enterprise, so on Service Hub Pro we build this using Custom Properties + Saved Views in CRM.)

1. How to Set It Up (First-Time Build)
  1. Go to Settings > Properties > Company Properties and create two dropdown fields: CSG Client Health Score (Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical) and Project Milestone Status (Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Delivered, Phase 2 Delivered, Final Presentation Delivered).
  2. Go to CRM > Companies, click + Add view, filter by CSG Client Health Score is any of Yellow, Red, and save it as At-Risk / Follow-Up Clients.
2. How to Use It (Ongoing CS Workflow)
  1. Review CRM > Companies with the Operations Director to verify every past/active client has linked Contacts (Contact Type = Past Client or Active Client) and past project Deals.
  2. Update the client's Health Score, write a 3-bullet status summary in Notes, and click Pin so incoming teams see it immediately.
If blocked or missing: Your Service Hub Pro Seat already includes full CRM access—you do not need a Sales Seat or extra Core Seat. If Settings > Properties or editing Companies/Deals is greyed out, ask your Super-Admin to enable Edit property settings and CRM Edit permissions (Companies & Deals) under Settings > Users & Teams.

HubSpot Menu: Service > Feedback Surveys

Feedback Surveys (NPS, CSAT & Past Survey Import)

What do I want to do? Send post-project NPS/CSAT surveys to clients and import past survey results.

−

What this tool does: Sends branded satisfaction surveys (NPS 0–10 loyalty scale or CSAT project ratings) and saves every score and testimonial quote directly onto the client's CRM record.

1. How to Set It Up (First-Time Build)
  1. Go to Service > Feedback Surveys > Create survey, choose NPS or CSAT, add the CSG logo, and select Email or Shareable link.
  2. Set the trigger to send automatically 3 days after a Deal moves to Closed Won / Completed, and add a follow-up workflow: if NPS ≥ 9, send a thank-you email asking for a testimonial quote.
  3. To import old Excel/Qualtrics surveys: create Contact properties (Historical NPS Score, Past Project CSAT Rating, Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote) under Settings > Properties.
2. How to Use It (Ongoing CS Workflow)
  1. Share the survey link after final client presentations or verify that the automated post-project email triggered.
  2. For past surveys, save your spreadsheet as .csv (UTF-8) with an Email column and upload via CRM > Contacts > Import.
If blocked or missing: Creating surveys requires your Service Hub Pro Seat. If Import is greyed out, ask your Super-Admin to enable CRM Import permissions under Settings > Users & Teams. (Tip: Imported CSV scores appear on Contact records/Custom Reports rather than HubSpot's native survey chart.)

HubSpot Menu: Service > Help Desk

Help Desk (Client Outreach & Shared Team Inbox)

What do I want to do? Run client outreach, testimonial follow-ups, and check-ins in one shared inbox.

−

What this tool does: Replaces private student email threads with a shared team workspace where every client outreach message and reply is logged as a ticket on the Company record.

1. How to Set It Up (First-Time Build)
  1. Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk → click Connect a channel > Team Email (e.g., client-support@...).
  2. Configure ticket stages: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved (and optional SLA response reminders).
2. How to Use It (Ongoing CS Workflow)
  1. Open Service > Help Desk, click Create ticket (e.g., Past Client Check-In & Testimonial), assign yourself as Ticket Owner, and link the Company & Contact.
  2. Send outreach emails directly from the ticket so replies stay visible to future cohorts, and use the yellow Comment tab with @Name to tag the Operations Director.
If blocked or missing: Requires your Service Hub Pro Seat. If connecting the shared team email fails or you cannot reply from tickets, ask a Super-Admin to connect the team inbox under Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk and add your user to the Help Desk team.
