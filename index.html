The Golden Data Rule: 4 Linked CRM Objects

How Information Connects

Contacts (People)

Individual people (CEOs, VPs, Alumni) tracked by email and tagged via Contact Type (Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, CSG Alumni).

Companies (Clients)

The client business (e.g., Alcomy). Holds the Client Health Score and links all people, projects, and notes together.

Deals (Projects)

Each consulting engagement from Discovery Call to Closed Won (scope, budget, semester, and final deliverables).

Tickets (Outreach & Support)

Tracks client follow-ups, data requests, and scope questions inside the shared Help Desk inbox.

2 Golden Rules for Every Consultant: (1) Always check if a Contact/Company already exists before creating a new one (email address is the unique ID). (2) After every client call, write a 3-bullet summary in Notes and click Pin so the next semester sees it immediately.

Marketing Hub How-Tos

What Your Marketing Seats Let You Do (Marketing Hub Pro)

CSG has 3 Marketing Core Seats and 2,000 Marketing Contacts. Here is what you can run:

  • Forms & Landing Pages: Capture inbound project leads directly into the CRM without manual typing.
  • Email & Automated Workflows: Send branded CSG newsletters and auto-send our Capabilities Deck after form fills.
  • Marketing Studio & Campaigns: Plan multi-channel campaigns (email + social + ads) in one place with Breeze AI.
  • Social, SEO & AEO: Schedule LinkedIn posts and optimize our site so Google and AI tools (ChatGPT/Gemini) recommend CSG.
What do I want to do?

"I want to run a marketing campaign across email and LinkedIn"

−

Concept: Group all emails, forms, social posts, and landing pages under one Campaign so CSG can track how many real leads each effort generated.

  1. Go to Marketing > Campaigns (or Marketing > Marketing Studio) and click Create campaign.
  2. Name it clearly by semester and goal (e.g., Fall 2026 – Tech Startup Outreach) and set your target audience.
  3. Attach your campaign assets (forms, emails, LinkedIn posts) using the Add assets button.
  4. Review influenced contacts and new deals directly inside the campaign dashboard.

What do I want to do?

"I want to build a website form, email, or automated follow-up"

−

Concept: Replace static email links with HubSpot forms that auto-save leads and immediately send CSG's Capabilities Deck.

  1. Build Form: Go to Marketing > Forms > Create form, include First Name, Last Name, Email, Company Name, Project Scope, and publish.
  2. Build Email: Go to Marketing > Email > Create email (choose Automated if triggered by a form) and add your copy + Capabilities PDF link.
  3. Connect Automation: Go to Automations > Workflows → set trigger Form submission = Website Inquiry → action Send email + Notify BD Lead.

  3. For student applicants or non-emailed contacts, select them in CRM > Contacts and click More > Set as non-marketing contacts.

Sales Hub How-Tos

Pick what you want to do, follow the short steps, and use the links to learn more.

What Your Sales Seats Let You Do (Sales Hub Pro)

Sales Seats give Business Development and Sales Leads everything needed to turn prospects into signed engagements:

  • Deal Pipelines & Stage Gates: Track every potential project from Discovery Call to Closed Won.
  • Meeting Scheduling Links: Let prospects book 20-minute strategy calls straight onto your calendar.
  • Document & Proposal Tracking: Upload Capabilities Decks/Proposals and get alerts when clients open them.
  • Outbound Sequences & Tasks: Automate multi-step follow-up emails and never drop a warm lead.
What do I want to do?

"I want to log a new prospect and track a deal through our pipeline"

−

Concept: Every potential client project must be a Deal linked to a Company and Contact so leadership sees accurate semester pipeline.

  1. Go to CRM > Deals and click Create deal (top right).
  2. Name it [Company Name] – [Semester/Scope], select the CSG Pipeline, enter the deal amount, and set the expected close date.
  3. Link the Company and decision-maker Contact under Associations, then drag the deal card across stages as it progresses.

What do I want to do?

"I want a prospect to book a Discovery Call without back-and-forth emails"

−

Concept: HubSpot Meeting Links sync with your calendar (replaces Calendly) and automatically log booked calls onto the Contact record.

  1. Go to Library > Meetings (or Sales > Meetings) and click Create scheduling page.
  2. Set duration to 20 or 30 minutes, connect Zoom/Teams, and set your available weekly hours.
  3. Copy your booking link and paste it into email signatures, outreach emails, and the website chatflow.
Helpful Guides & Academy Links

What do I want to do?

"I want to send a Capabilities Deck or Proposal and see when clients read it"

−

Concept: Uploading PDFs to HubSpot Documents gives you real-time alerts when a prospect opens your deck and shows which pages they read longest.

  1. Go to Library > Documents and click Upload document (upload the CSG Capabilities Deck or Scope Proposal PDF).
  2. Click Share to generate a trackable link (require email address so you know who viewed it).
  3. Follow up within 24 hours of receiving an open notification.
Helpful Guides & Academy Links

What do I want to do?

"I want to send automated outbound follow-up sequences to leads"

−

Concept: Sequences send a timed series of 1-to-1 follow-up emails and task reminders—and automatically stop the moment the prospect replies.

  1. Go to Automations > Sequences and click Create sequence.
  2. Add 3–4 short, personalized email steps spaced 3 business days apart + a LinkedIn/call task reminder.
  3. Open any Contact record and click Enroll in sequence.

Written for each team's Customer Success Lead & the Operations Director.

Going forward, each marketing team has one dedicated Customer Success person working directly with the Operations Director. With the Service Hub Pro Seat, your job is to keep our past and active client database alive, run client outreach, and collect NPS & testimonial proof points:

What do I want to do? • Database & Account Health

"I want to collect & update all past clients, projects, and contacts with the Operations Director"

−

Why & Concept: Prevents semester brain drain by keeping every past and active client's contacts, project history, and relationship health (Green / Yellow / Red) accurate in one place. (Note: On Service Hub Pro, we track health via custom Company properties + Saved Views).

  1. Verify Properties (One-Time Setup): Under Settings > Properties > Company Properties, ensure CSG Client Health Score (Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical) and Project Milestone Status (Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Delivered, Phase 2 Delivered, Final Presentation Delivered) exist.
  2. Audit & Update Past Clients: Open CRM > Companies with the Operations Director. Check each past/active client record to confirm key Contacts (tagged Contact Type = Past Client or Active Client) and past project Deals are linked.
  3. Log Status & Pin Notes: Update the client's Health Score and Milestone Status, then write a 3-bullet summary in Notes and click Pin so it stays at the top.
  4. Monitor Risk View: Open your saved view Active / At-Risk CSG Clients (filtered by Health Score = Yellow or Red) and assign a follow-up Task to the Operations Director if a client needs attention.

What do I want to do? • NPS, CSAT & Past Survey Import

"I want to send NPS / feedback surveys to clients & import past survey data"

−

Why & Concept: Centralizes client satisfaction scores (CSAT & NPS) and quotable testimonials on Contact/Company records so Marketing has real proof points for case studies and the website.

  1. Create Automated Survey: Go to Service > Feedback Surveys > Create survey, pick NPS (0–10 recommendation scale) or CSAT, add the CSG logo, and select Email or Shareable link.
  2. Automate Post-Project Send & Testimonials: Set the survey to send automatically 3 days after a Deal moves to Closed Won / Completed (or at semester end). Add a workflow: if NPS ≥ 9, send a thank-you email asking for a website testimonial quote.
  3. Import Past Surveys (Excel / Google Forms / Qualtrics): Create custom Contact properties (Historical NPS Score, Past Project CSAT Rating, Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote, Survey Completion Date), save your spreadsheet as .csv (UTF-8) with an Email column, and upload via CRM > Contacts > Import.

What do I want to do? • Client Outreach & Shared Inbox

"I want to run client outreach, check-ins & project follow-ups in one shared workspace"

−

Why & Concept: Instead of losing client replies in personal student inboxes, Help Desk logs every outreach thread, data request, and follow-up as a ticket directly on the client's Company record.

  1. Connect Shared Inbox (Setup): Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk → click Connect a channel > Team Email (e.g., client-support@...) and set pipeline stages: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved.
  2. Log Outreach or Client Requests: Open Service > Help Desk, click Create ticket (e.g., Alcomy – Post-Project Check-In & Testimonial), assign yourself as Ticket Owner, and link the Company & Contact.
  3. Reply & Collaborate Internally: Send emails directly from the ticket window so the full thread stays in CRM. Use the yellow Comment (Internal Note) tab with @Name to coordinate privately with the Operations Director or Paul Godfrey.

What do I want to do? • Satisfaction Metrics & Dashboards

"I want to pull NPS scores, client satisfaction stats & response metrics for Marketing & Leadership"

−

Why & Concept: Turns client feedback and response times into visual charts that prove CSG's impact to Paul Godfrey and show Marketing which client segments give the highest NPS scores.

  1. Go to Service > Service Analytics and open Customer Outcomes (NPS & CSAT trends) or Response Times.
  2. Filter the date range to the current or past semester, then click Save report → Add to existing dashboard (CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard).
  3. To automate monthly updates: open Reporting > Dashboards → click Share > Email this dashboard → schedule a recurring monthly PDF to Paul Godfrey and team leads.
